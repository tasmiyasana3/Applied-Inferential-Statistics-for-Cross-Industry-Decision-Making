# Statistical Analysis Business Case Studies

## Project Overview
This project provides a detailed statistical analysis of various real-world business scenarios in industries like sports rehabilitation, manufacturing, stone printing, and dental healthcare technology.

The analysis uses probability concepts, hypothesis testing, statistical distributions, t-tests, ANOVA, and exploratory data analysis (EDA) to produce insights and recommendations powered by data.

## Business Problems Covered

### 1. Football Player Injury Analysis
We examined the link between player positions and foot injuries among professional football players.

Key objectives:
- Calculate injury probabilities
- Identify high-risk player positions
- Understand injury distribution across roles

### 2. Gunny Bags Strength Analysis
We conducted a statistical analysis on the breaking strength of gunny bags used for cement packaging.

Key objectives:
- Evaluate quality consistency
- Identify weak and excessively strong bags
- Assess operational and supply chain risks

### 3. Zingaro Stone Hardness Analysis
We analyzed the Brinell Hardness Index (BHI) values of polished and unpolished stones to determine their suitability for printing.

Key objectives:
- Determine if unpolished stones meet hardness requirements
- Compare hardness levels between polished and unpolished stones
- Use hypothesis testing with t-tests

### 4. Dental Alloy Hardness Study
We studied how implant hardness varies based on:
- Dentists
- Implant methods
- Alloy types
- Temperature treatment

This project includes interaction effect analysis and ANOVA testing.

## Key Insights

### Football Injury Analysis
- About 62% of football players had injuries.
- Strikers faced the highest injury risk among all positions.
- Forwards and wingers made up more than half of the player population.

### Gunny Bags Strength Analysis
- Approximately 11% of gunny bags were weaker than acceptable levels.
- About 14% of bags were outside the optimal strength range.
- Quality inconsistencies could lead to supply chain losses and higher production costs.

### Zingaro Stone Analysis
- Unpolished stones had much lower hardness values than needed for printing.
- Polished stones were more suitable for printing operations.
- Statistical tests confirmed significant hardness differences between polished and unpolished stones.

### Dental Alloy Study
- Implant hardness varied greatly based on dentists and methods.
- Alloy type and treatment temperature affected implant durability.
- We observed interaction effects between dentists and methods.

---

## Business Recommendations

### Sports Rehabilitation
- Develop position-specific injury prevention programs.
- Monitor workload and fatigue in high-risk player groups.

### Manufacturing Quality Control
- Enhance stitching and material quality for gunny bags.
- Conduct regular quality inspections.

### Stone Printing Industry
- Use polished stones for premium printing operations.
- Implement strict BHI screening protocols before printing.

### Dental Healthcare
- Standardize implant procedures across dentists.
- Optimize combinations of alloy and method for better durability.
